### Reaction Protocol

|     | SM30 | SM30 | eGFP | ARG | FCB |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Template | 2.5uL | 2.7uL | 6uL | Messi | Messi |
| 2x NTP/CAP | 10  | 10  | 10  | Messi | Messi |
| 10x Reaction Buffer | 2   | 2   | 2   | Messi | Messi |
| Enzyme | 2   | 2   | 2   | Messi | Messi |
| H<sub>2</sub>O | 3.5 | 3.3 | 0   | Messi | Messi |

<ins>Vortex reaction buffer and 2x CAP</ins>

Reaction mix (Above table)

Mix by flicking (no vortex)

Incubate at 37 °C for 4 hrs

1uL Turbo DNA mix

Incubate at 37 °C for 15 min

Poly-A tailing reaction

Clean the table again

### Reaction mix (same for all 3 samples)

| Reaction from previous reaction | 10 uL |
| --- | --- |
| H<sub>2</sub>O | 18uL |
| 5x E-PAP Buffer | 10 uL |
| 25mM MnCl<sub>2</sub> | 5 uL |
| 10mM ATP | 5 uL |
| E-PAP enzyme | 2 uL |

Make the reaction mix

Incubate at 37 °C for 1 hr

Run in the jel

Precipitation of the mRNA

30 uL of LICl<sub>2</sub> , overnight in -20

Centrifuge at full speed for 30 min

Clear the sup.

150 uL of 70% EtOH, centrifuge at full speed for 15 min

Repeat the above process

Air dry on ice

20 uL of nuclease-free H<sub>2</sub>O

Measure conc.

SM30:GFP : 1552.9 ng/uL

SM50:GFP: 1648.2 ng/uL

eGFP: 2838.1 ng/uL
